HOW TO USE:
-----------
1. Open http://localhost:3000/ in your browser
2. Click "Play" to start the simulation
3. Watch the 3D aircraft turnaround process
4. Select different flights from the dropdown to compare
5. Observe how temperature affects:
   - Staff efficiency
   - Buffer requirements
   - Delay costs
   - On-time performance

KEY OBSERVATIONS:
-----------------
- Low Temperature (30-35°C): Static buffer (15 min) is sufficient
- Medium Temperature (36-44°C): Dynamic buffer increases to 18-25 min
- High Temperature (45-50°C): Dynamic buffer reaches 30-40 min
- Peak heat hours (12:00-15:00): Maximum delays and costs
- Staff fatigue increases with temperature
- GSE reliability decreases in extreme heat
